Disclaimer

The PSLE Prep Toolkit is an independent study resource and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Botswana Examinations Council (BEC) or the Ministry of Education and Skills Development. “PSLE” refers only to the exam this platform helps learners prepare for. Content is based on publicly available syllabus outlines and is meant to supplement, not replace, school teaching and official materials.

No guarantee of results

Practising with this toolkit does not guarantee any particular exam result, grade or place at a secondary school. Our questions are written to reflect the style and level of the PSLE, but they are not past papers and they are not the questions your child will sit. Exam performance depends on many things beyond any study resource.

About the content

All questions and explanations are original material written for this toolkit, based on publicly available syllabus outlines for Standard 6 and 7. We do not reproduce copyrighted textbooks or examination papers.

We check our content carefully, but with over two thousand questions some errors are inevitable. The composition feedback tool judges structure, length and accuracy only — it cannot mark imagination or ideas, and it is not a substitute for a teacher reading your child's work.

If you or your child's teacher spot a mistake, please tell us on WhatsApp 74310425. We correct errors as quickly as we can and we are grateful for them being pointed out.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your child's date of birth, home address, identity number, school name or photograph. We do not ask for or store your card details, PIN or mobile money password — payments happen entirely through your own bank or mobile money service, never through this website. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Children's information

Accounts are bought and controlled by a parent or guardian, not by the child. We ask only for a learner's first name and standard, so the app can greet them and keep their practice separate from a sibling's. A child never enters personal information into this toolkit, and their scores are never shown to other learners or ranked against them by name.

Who can see it

Only the owner of The Bright Minds, in order to confirm payments and provide support. Access to our records requires a password and a second verification code. Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form that nobody — including us — can read.

Data Protection

Where it is kept

Your information is held in a secured database provided by Supabase, and the website is delivered over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Because these are international services, your information may be stored on servers outside Botswana. We keep it only as described here and never for advertising.

How long we keep it

Access records are kept while your access is valid and for a reasonable period afterwards, so that we can help if you return or query a payment. You may ask us to delete your details at any time.
