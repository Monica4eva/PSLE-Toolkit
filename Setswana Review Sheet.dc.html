Tsela ya go tlhatlhoba / How to mark
Karabo e e tshwailweng e bonwa ka ✓ mme e kwadilwe ka mokwalo o mokima. Bala potso, netefatsa gore ✓ e mo karabong e e siameng, mme o lebe gore tlhaloso e ruta sentle.

The marked answer is shown in bold with a ✓. Ring anything wrong and write the correction in the margin. Please note the question number (e.g. SET-042) — that is what we use to fix it. If the same mistake repeats across many questions, one note about the pattern is more useful than marking every one.
